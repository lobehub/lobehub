# How to Top Up Credits

<CloudOnlyAlert />

LobeHub Cloud users can purchase additional credits to continue using the built-in model service. Credit top-ups are available to both free and paid users, with no plan upgrade required.

## Buy Credits

<Steps>
  ### Open App Settings

  Click your profile avatar in the top-left corner of the homepage, then click **App Settings**.

  ### Open Credits

  In the left navigation, find the **Plans** section and click **Credits**.

  The top of the page shows your top-up credit balance and subscription credits. Click **View Usage** to open the usage page, or **Top-up History** to review purchases.

  ### Select a Credit Amount

  In **Buy Credits**, select one of the packages shown on the page. You can also select **Custom** and enter an amount. Custom purchases start at 10M credits.

  The page shows the credit amount, price, bonus credits, and validity period. Unit prices can vary by plan; use the price shown on the purchase page.

  ### Complete the Purchase

  Review the total, click **Buy Now**, and follow the checkout prompts to complete payment.
</Steps>

## Automatic Top-Up

Automatic top-up can purchase credits when your balance runs low. Before enabling it, click **Add Payment Method** and save a bank card, then follow the page prompts to configure the top-up rules.

## Review Credit Packages

**My Credit Packages** lists each package's remaining balance, source, acquisition date, and expiration date. Switch between **Active, Used Up**, and **Expired**, or filter and sort by source and date.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long are credits valid?

Use the date shown on the purchase page and under **My Credit Packages**. The current purchase page shows a **six-month** validity period for top-up credits.

### How are credits consumed?

LobeHub uses subscription credits before top-up credits. Expiration is calculated separately for each top-up package.

### Can free users buy and use top-up credits?

Yes. Both free and paid users can purchase and use top-up credits.

### Do credit prices vary by plan?

Unit prices can vary by plan. Before payment, the purchase area shows the unit price, bonus credits, validity period, and total for the current account.
