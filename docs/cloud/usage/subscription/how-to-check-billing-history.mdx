# How to View Billing History in LobeHub

<CloudOnlyAlert />

The **Billing** page shows your current plan's validity and benefits, provides access to payment management, and lists previous orders.

## Open Billing

<Steps>
  ### Open App Settings

  Click your profile avatar in the top-left corner of the homepage, then click **App Settings**.

  ### Open Billing

  In the left navigation, find the **Plans** section and click **Billing**.
</Steps>

## Review the Billing Summary

The **Billing Summary** at the top shows how long the current plan remains valid. You can also:

- Click **View This Month's Usage** to open Usage.
- Click **Upgrade Plan** to review other subscription plans.
- Click **Payment Management** to manage payment information.

For a one-time payment plan, the page indicates that it will not renew or charge automatically.

## Review the Current Plan

The **Current Plan** section shows the active plan, expiration date, and key benefits, such as monthly credits, file storage, vector storage, and available features.

The expiration notice shows when the subscription ends so you can plan usage or choose another plan before that date.

## Review Billing History

**Billing History** lists:

| Item | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Order Number** | Identifier for the transaction |
| **Transaction Status** | For example, paid, unpaid, or closed |
| **Amount** | Actual amount for the order |
| **Payment Date** | Date when payment was completed |

Click **View** on the right side of an order to open its billing details.
