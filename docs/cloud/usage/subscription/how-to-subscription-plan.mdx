# How to Subscribe to a Plan in LobeHub

<CloudOnlyAlert />

LobeHub has three paid plans for individuals:

- **Starter**: For occasional AI users.
- **Premium**: For professional users who use AI frequently.
- **Ultimate**: For heavy users who need complex AI conversations.

Higher tiers include more monthly credits, file storage, and vector storage. Check the **Plans** page for current prices, complete benefits, and available billing cycles.

<Steps>
  ### Open **App Settings**

  On the LobeHub home page, click your profile avatar in the top-left corner, then click **App Settings**.

  ### Open the **Plans** Page

  In the App Settings sidebar, find the **Plans** section and click **Plans**.

  ### Choose a Plan and Billing Cycle

  Compare the credits, file and knowledge base storage, and other benefits included with each plan. Then choose monthly or annual billing.

  ### Complete Your Subscription

  Click the subscribe or upgrade button for your preferred plan, then follow the page instructions to complete payment. After payment succeeds, the new plan appears as your current plan.
</Steps>
