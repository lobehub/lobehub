# Earn Credits by Inviting Friends

<CloudOnlyAlert />

Invite a friend to LobeHub. After they join through your referral, complete their first qualifying payment, and pass review, both of you receive **2M credits**.

## Open Referral Rewards

<Steps>
  ### Open App Settings

  Click your profile avatar in the top-left corner of the homepage, then click **App Settings**.

  ### Open Referral Rewards

  In the left navigation, find the **Plans** section and click **Referral Rewards**.

  The page shows your referral link, referral code, overview, referral records, and program rules.
</Steps>

## Invite a Friend

Copy your **referral link** and share it with a friend. You can also copy your **referral code** for them to enter during registration.

A qualifying referral follows these steps:

1. Your friend registers through your referral link or enters your code on the registration page.
2. Your friend completes their first qualifying payment, such as a subscription or personal credit purchase.
3. After the payment passes review, both of you receive **2M credits**.

<Callout type="info">
  Rewards are issued after the qualifying payment passes review, not immediately after registration.
</Callout>

## Add a Referral Code After Registration

A friend can add the code within **seven days** after registration:

- If they have already completed a qualifying payment and passed review, the reward is issued after the code is linked.
- If they have not paid yet, the reward is issued after their first qualifying payment passes review.

## Review Referral Results

The **Referral Overview** shows:

- Total invitations
- Rewarded referrals
- Total rewards
- Available balance

Use **Referral Records** to review the status and reward for each invitation.

## Credit Order and Expiration

Referral credits are consumed in this order: **free credits → subscription credits → referral credits → top-up credits**.

Referral credits are cleared after **100 days** of account inactivity.

## Program Rules

- A referral qualifies only after the invited friend registers and completes a qualifying payment.
- Abuse such as bulk registrations with temporary email addresses can result in permanent account suspension.
- Reward amounts and program rules can change. Use the Referral Rewards page for the current terms.
