# How to Change Subscription Plans in LobeHub

<CloudOnlyAlert />

You can compare plans in App Settings and change the current subscription to a higher plan. To use a lower plan, cancel the current subscription, wait for it to expire, and then subscribe to the lower plan.

## Open Plans

<Steps>
  ### Open App Settings

  Click your profile avatar in the top-left corner of the homepage, then click **App Settings**.

  ### Open Plans

  In the left navigation, find the **Plans** section and click **Plans**.

  The page shows your current plan, available plans, monthly credits, storage, and other benefits. Switch between **Monthly** and **Yearly** to review the corresponding prices.
</Steps>

## Upgrade Your Plan

<Steps>
  ### Select a Higher Plan

  Find the higher plan you want and click the purchase or upgrade button shown on the page.

  ### Review the Change

  Check the target plan, billing cycle, effective date, and amount due. The remaining value of your current plan may be applied as a discount. Use the amount shown on the confirmation page.

  ### Complete the Upgrade

  Follow the page prompts to complete payment. The result page shows whether the upgrade succeeded and when it takes effect.
</Steps>

An upgrade usually takes effect after payment succeeds. If the page allows a change from monthly to yearly billing, the confirmation page shows the amount due and effective date.

<Callout type="info">
  If the current subscription has an unpaid invoice, pay the invoice or cancel that subscription before upgrading.
</Callout>

## Change to a Lower Plan

You cannot change directly to a lower plan while the current subscription is active. In the **Current Plan** section, click **Manage Subscription** to cancel the current subscription, then wait for the plan to expire.

After the account moves to the Free plan, return to Plans and subscribe to the lower plan you want. You can continue using the current plan's benefits until expiration.

If cancellation has not taken effect, you can click **Restore Subscription** before expiration to continue automatic renewal.

## Subscriptions Purchased on Mobile

If you purchased the subscription through a mobile app, the web page directs you to manage it on your mobile device. Change or cancel the subscription through the original purchase platform.
