# Welcome to LobeHub

**The ultimate space for work and life** — find, build, and collaborate with Agent teammates that grow with you.

![LobeHub](https://hub-apac-1.lobeobjects.space/landing/images/home/overview-light.webp)

You've probably been here: you ask one AI to draft a report, paste the output into another to polish the tone, then copy that into a third tool for formatting. None of them remember what the others did. None of them remember what *you* did last week. Every conversation starts from scratch, every context is lost, and you're the one stitching it all together.

LobeHub is built for people who are tired of that loop. Here, your Agents share context, remember your preferences, work together in teams, and get better over time — so you spend less time repeating yourself and more time on work that matters.

## Create — Start with a single sentence

You don't need to configure a model, write a system prompt, or connect an API. Just describe what you need — "a writing assistant that matches my brand voice" or "a code reviewer who knows our stack" — and LobeHub assembles the right model, Skills, and context into a ready-to-run Agent.

Start bare-bones. Then layer in a knowledge base when your Agent needs domain expertise, add web search when it needs real-time data, or plug in MCP tools when it needs to take action. Your Agent grows as your work grows.

Not sure where to begin? Browse the [Community](/docs/usage/community/agent-market) — thousands of ready-made Agents are waiting. Find one close to what you need, install it, and make it yours.

## Collaborate — Your Agents work as a team

Imagine briefing a research analyst, a copywriter, and a fact-checker on the same project — except they're all Agents, they share the same context, and they work in parallel without you managing the hand-offs.

That's what [Groups](/docs/usage/agent/agent-team) do. Multiple specialized Agents collaborate on a single task — debating approaches, iterating on outputs, and refining results together. You set the goal; they coordinate the execution.

Beyond Groups, LobeHub weaves Agents into your daily workflow:

- **[Pages](/docs/usage/agent/pages)** — Writing a proposal at midnight? Bring in multiple Agents as co-editors, all sharing the same context. One drafts, another critiques, a third checks the data. The result is a polished document, not a pile of chat messages.
- **Scheduled Tasks** — Need a competitor report every Monday morning? Set it once. Your Agent compiles, summarizes, and delivers it — even while you sleep.
- **Workspace** — Your team and your Agents share one space. Everyone sees the same projects, the same context, the same progress.

## Evolve — They remember, they learn, they adapt

Most AI tools have the memory of a goldfish. You explain your coding style, your preferred tone, your company's naming conventions — and next session, it's all gone. You start over. Again.

LobeHub Agents don't forget. They build a structured understanding of how you work — your preferences, your patterns, your domain knowledge — and that [Memory](/docs/usage/getting-started/memory) carries across every conversation. The more you work together, the less you need to explain.

And unlike black-box systems, this Memory is fully yours: structured, editable, and transparent. You see exactly what your Agent remembers, you edit what's wrong, you delete what's outdated. Your Agent's reasoning unfolds step-by-step through Chain of Thought — so you always know *why* it made a decision, not just *what* it decided.

## Choose the right model for every task

Not every question needs the most powerful model. A quick translation? A lightweight model handles it in milliseconds for a fraction of the cost. A nuanced legal analysis? Route it to the model with the deepest reasoning capabilities.

LobeHub gives you access to **70+ providers** — GPT-4o, Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek, Llama, Qwen, and more — from over a dozen providers. Switch models mid-conversation, or let your Agent pick the best one for each turn. You can even run local models if your data stays on-device.

Each Agent can have its own default model, so your code reviewer uses one model while your copywriter uses another — automatically, without you making a choice each time.

## 10,000+ Skills — connect Agents to the tools you use

An Agent that can only chat is only half the story. With [Skills](/docs/usage/agent/skills), your Agent reads your GitHub issues, queries your database, sends a Slack message, generates a PDF, or books a meeting — all within the same conversation.

LobeHub supports the [Model Context Protocol (MCP)](/docs/usage/community/mcp-market), an open standard that connects Agents to an ever-growing ecosystem of **10,000+ tools**. Install a Skill from the marketplace in one click, or build your own and share it with the Community.

A few real-world examples:

- *"Summarize the top 5 GitHub issues and post a digest to Slack"* — your Agent reads the issues, writes the summary, sends the message.
- *"Pull this week's sales data and build a chart"* — your Agent queries the database, processes the numbers, renders a visual Artifact.
- *"Find flights to Tokyo next month under $800"* — your Agent searches, compares, and presents options with booking links.

## Resource Library — give your Agents real knowledge

Generic AI answers are fine for generic questions. But when you need an Agent that knows *your* codebase, *your* product docs, *your* company policies — that's where the [Resource Library](/docs/usage/agent/resources) comes in.

Upload documents, images, spreadsheets, audio, or video. Your Agent references them during conversations, so its answers are grounded in your actual data — not just training-set knowledge. Build a knowledge base for each Agent, or share resources across your Workspace.

Imagine a customer support Agent that knows your entire help center, a legal assistant trained on your contract templates, or a product manager who has read every PRD you've ever written. That's Resource Library at work.

## Voice — go hands-free

Sometimes you need your Agents while your hands are busy — commuting, cooking, walking through a warehouse. LobeHub supports text-to-speech and speech-to-text, so you can have full conversations without typing a word.

Dictate a meeting summary on your walk back to the office. Have your Agent read a long analysis out loud while you drive. Or just talk through an idea and let your Agent capture the structure.

## Create visuals without leaving the conversation

Need a hero image for a blog post? A diagram for your architecture doc? A quick mockup to pitch an idea? Ask your Agent — it generates images using DALL-E 3, fal.ai, and other models, and renders them as Artifacts right in the chat.

No need to switch to a separate design tool, export, download, and re-upload. The image is there, in context, next to the conversation that produced it.

## Community — shared intelligence

LobeHub's philosophy starts with people. The [Community](/docs/usage/community/agent-market) is where thousands of creators publish Agents, Skills, and workflows for everyone to discover, install, and remix.

Found an Agent that's 80% of what you need? Install it, tweak the prompt, swap the model, add your own knowledge base — and it becomes yours. Or build something from scratch and share it back. The best ideas compound when they're open.

## Make it yours

LobeHub adapts to how you like to work — not the other way around.

- **[Appearance](/docs/usage/user-interface/appearance)** — Light mode, dark mode, custom accent colors, and layout density. Make your workspace feel like home.
- **[Keyboard Shortcuts](/docs/usage/user-interface/shortcuts)** — Power users can navigate, switch conversations, and trigger actions without touching the mouse.
- **[Command Menu](/docs/usage/user-interface/command-menu)** — Press a key and search everything — Agents, conversations, settings, actions — all from one place.
