# Welcome to LobeHub

LobeHub is the workspace for work and life: find, build, and collaborate with Agent teammates that grow with you. We're building the first human–Agent co-working, co-evolving network.

![LobeHub](https://hub-apac-1.lobeobjects.space/landing/images/home/overview-light.webp)

## Why LobeHub?

Most AI agents today are task-driven tools — they lack Context, live in isolation, and require manual hand-offs. Even when they retain Memory, it's often global, shallow, and impersonal. You end up toggling between fragmented conversations with no structured way to build on past work.

LobeHub treats **Agents as the unit of work**. Your Agents maintain structured, editable Memory, collaborate in Groups, and adapt to act at the right moment — across your entire workflow.

## Core Principles

### Create — Agents as the unit of work

Describe what you need once, and LobeHub assembles the right model and Skills into a ready-to-run Agent. Start simple, add tools and knowledge as your work demands.

- **Any model, any modality** — Seamlessly access 70+ models including GPT-4o, Claude, Gemini, and local LLMs, all under your control
- **10,000+ Skills** — Connect your Agents to tools and MCP-compatible integrations
- **Personal Memory** — Your Agents build a structured understanding of your needs through continual learning

### Collaborate — at scale

Groups bring multiple specialized Agents together, enabling parallel collaboration and iterative improvement — like a real team.

- **Pages** — Write and refine content with multiple Agents in one place with shared Context
- **Scheduled Tasks** — Let Agents run automated workflows at the right time, even while you're away
- **Workspace** — Shared spaces for teams to collaborate with Agents across projects

### Evolve — humans and Agents co-evolve

The best Agents are ones that understand how you work. LobeHub features Memory that builds a clear picture of your preferences and adapts over time.

- **Continual learning** — Your Agents learn from how you work, getting sharper with every interaction
- **White-box Memory** — Structured and editable — you have full control over what Agents remember
- **Transparency** — Watch reasoning unfold step-by-step with Chain of Thought visualization
