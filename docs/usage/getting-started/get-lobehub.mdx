# Getting LobeHub

LobeHub runs wherever you work — in the browser, on your desktop, or on your phone. Sign in with the same account and your Agents, Pages, and Memory sync across all devices.

## Use LobeHub in Your Browser

No installation required. Open [app.lobehub.com](https://app.lobehub.com) in any modern browser and start immediately. Your data syncs to the cloud, so you can switch devices anytime.

**Best for:** Quick access, trying LobeHub before installing, or using it on shared or restricted computers.

## Desktop Apps

### macOS

Download the native app from the [Download Page](https://lobehub.com/download). The app is not currently available on the Mac App Store.

**Benefits:** Offline access to your workspace, system integration, and a dedicated window for focused work.

### Windows

Download from the [Download Page](https://lobehub.com/download). The app is not currently available on the Microsoft Store.

**Benefits:** Same as macOS — a native, focused experience with offline support.

## Mobile Apps

### iOS

Download from the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6471212236). Sign in with your LobeHub account to access your Agents and conversations on the go.

**Best for:** Voice conversations, quick check-ins, and reviewing outputs while away from your desk.

### Android

Download from [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.lobehub.app), or get the APK from the [Download Page](https://lobehub.com/download) for manual installation.

**Best for:** Same as iOS — mobile access to your workspace wherever you are.
