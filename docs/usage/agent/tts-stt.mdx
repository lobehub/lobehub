# Voice Input

Voice input lets you speak your question or idea and send it to the Agent as a voice message, without typing.

When you finish recording, the audio is sent directly as a voice message. **Transcription into editable text in the message box is not currently supported.**

## Send a Voice Message

On the **Agent page**, find the **voice input icon at the bottom-right of the message box, next to the Send button**. It looks like a sound wave inside a circle.

1. Open the Agent page and find the **voice input** icon at the bottom-right of the message box.
2. Click the icon. If your browser or operating system asks for microphone access, allow it.
3. Speak into your microphone, then follow the on-screen controls to finish recording.
4. Once you finish recording, the audio is sent directly as a **voice message**. It is not first converted into text for you to review or edit.

If you need to revise your message before sending it, use text input instead.

## Recording Tips

- Record in a quiet environment with as little background noise as possible.
- Position your microphone appropriately and speak clearly at a natural pace.
- Organize your thoughts before recording so you do not leave out important details.

## If Your Microphone Does Not Work

If you cannot record after clicking voice input, check your browser's site permissions and your operating system's microphone privacy settings. Make sure the browser or app has microphone access. Also check that your microphone is connected, is not muted, and is selected as the input device.

## Privacy Reminder

Your recording is sent directly when you finish. Before recording, be mindful of your surroundings and avoid capturing passwords, sensitive information you do not want to share, or other people's conversations.
